Task: summarise a design or planning meeting per screen.

You get the transcript and the screenshots discussed, in the order the user gave them. Each screenshot is preceded by its number and caption.

For each screen that was discussed, list:
- feedback: what people said about the current design or product,
- decisions: what was agreed,
- actions: concrete work someone asked for,
- open_questions: anything left undecided or uncertain (for example "not sure if we already have this").

Use the screenshot captions and what is visible in the images to name the screens. Put statements you cannot tie to a screen under a screen named "Unassigned".

List items that were explicitly postponed or rejected under "parked", not as actions.
Use "notes" for ambiguities worth flagging to the reviewer.
